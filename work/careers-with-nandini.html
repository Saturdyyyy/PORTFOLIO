Careers With Nandini

A career-guidance platform built from scratch, with payments, bookings and automated communication.

Visit careerswithnandini.com ↗Book a similar project
RoleDesigner & developer
PlatformWeb
ScopeDesign, build, deploy, integrations
ToolsJavaScript, Supabase, Netlify

Challenge

A career-guidance creator needed one place to explain her offer, sell digital resources, take session bookings and keep in touch with her audience, without a development team.

My role

I designed and built the platform from scratch and deployed it on its own domain, including every integration.

Solution

  • Razorpay checkout for paid resources.
  • Topmate integration for one-to-one session bookings.
  • Email automation and broadcasting to keep the audience informed.
  • Custom UI animations and a responsive layout.
  • Supabase for data and Netlify for hosting.

Deliverables

  • Full website design and front end
  • Payment, booking and email integrations through their APIs
  • Deployment and domain setup

Tools

JavaScript, Supabase, Netlify, Razorpay, Topmate, Figma.

Outcome

A working platform delivered end to end, from the first design to live payments and bookings.
