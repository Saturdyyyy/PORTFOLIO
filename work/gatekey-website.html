GateKey Website

The marketing website for GateKey, designed and built front to back, plus the product visuals around it.

Visit gatekey.co.in ↗Book a similar project
RoleUI designer & front-end developer
PlatformWeb
ScopeWebsite, dashboard visuals, collateral
ToolsHTML, CSS, JS, GSAP, Lenis

Challenge

A software product for gated communities needed a website that explains how visitor check-in, approvals and reporting work, and makes the product look as reliable as it is.

My role

I designed the website UI and built the front end, and produced dashboard visuals, product graphics, banners and device mockups for marketing.

Solution

  • A clear page flow from the problem to features, workflow, pricing and contact.
  • Scroll-driven animation with GSAP and smooth scrolling with Lenis, kept light enough for phones.
  • A responsive layout tested down to small mobile screens.
  • Product visuals that show the real workflow instead of stock imagery.

Deliverables

  • Website UI design and front-end build
  • Dashboard visuals and device mockups
  • Banners and marketing collateral

Tools

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GSAP, Lenis, Figma.

Outcome

A live marketing website at gatekey.co.in, with matching product visuals for sales and social.
