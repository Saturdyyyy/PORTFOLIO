About

Designer's eye. Developer's hands.

I'm Harsh T, a Computer Science student at JECRC University in Jaipur and an independent developer and designer. I take products from the first sketch to a live URL, and I care about the parts in between: clear interfaces, honest copy and code that is easy to hand over.

How I work

I start with the outcome a project needs, then design the smallest version that achieves it. I design in Figma before writing code, review work in stages and write everything down, so there are no surprises at handover.

I use AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Cursor every day, and I read and understand the code they produce. Speed is useful; knowing why something works is what keeps it working.

Where I'm headed

Alongside engineering, I plan to pursue an MBA after my B.Tech, to combine building products with the business strategy behind them.
