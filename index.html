FAQ

Questions clients ask.

What services do you offer?

Three kinds of work: Build (websites, web apps, landing pages, UI/UX and mobile product interfaces), Grow (brand identity, social creatives and content systems) and Automate (AI-assisted workflows for leads, follow-ups and reporting).

Do you build the backend too, or only the front end?

Both, where the project needs it. I build front ends in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, small backends in Node.js and Express, and connect services like Supabase, payment gateways and third-party APIs.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I am based in Jaipur, India and work with clients remotely. Calls are scheduled in IST with overlap for other time zones.

Who owns the work when it is finished?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the code, designs and accounts are handed over to you, along with the access details.

Can you help with AI automation?

Yes. I map one repetitive process first, then build and test a workflow with human review built in, using tools such as Notion, ChatGPT and Claude. Third-party subscription costs are disclosed up front.

What do I need to have ready?

A short description of the goal, any brand assets or content you already have, and access to your domain or hosting if they exist. I can help with the rest.

How long does a project take?

A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks and a full business website 3–6 weeks. Web apps and automation systems depend on scope. Every timeline is confirmed in writing in the proposal.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Optional. You can take the handover and run it yourself, or choose a monthly care plan for fixes, updates and small improvements.
