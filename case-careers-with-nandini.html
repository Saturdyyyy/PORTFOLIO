CASE STUDY 02 · END-TO-END BUILD

CAREERS WITH
NANDINI

A career-guidance brand website built and deployed end to end, with payments, bookings and a growing social media presence behind it.

RoleDeveloper, designer and social media manager
ScopeDesign, build, deploy, payments, content
StackJavaScript, Netlify, Supabase, Razorpay, Topmate
Livecareerswithnandini.com ↗

Overview

Careers With Nandini is a career-guidance brand. It needed a website that could explain the offer, take payments for digital playbooks and let people book one-to-one sessions, without a development team behind it.

My role

  • Designed and built the site, then deployed it to production on its own domain.
  • Integrated Razorpay checkout for the playbooks and Topmate for session bookings.
  • Set up Supabase for the data layer.

Beyond the build

Alongside the website, the same brand's social media runs through the same hands: PR, creator collaborations, email outreach, data handling, post design and script writing. Building the site and running the audience side meant decisions on the page were made with real content and real enquiries in mind.

Challenges

  • Payments: wiring a payment gateway correctly means handling success, failure and verification states, not just a button.
  • One person, many jobs: design, code, hosting and content had to be kept simple enough to maintain alone.

What I learned

  • Shipping end to end teaches the unglamorous parts: domains, environment variables, payment verification and monitoring.
  • Knowing the audience-side work makes the product-side choices better.
