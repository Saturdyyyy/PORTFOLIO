Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: details you send are used only to reply to you and are never sold or shared for marketing.

What is collected

Only what you type into the contact form or booking tool: your name, email, company, website, project details and preferences.

How it is used

To reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal and keep a simple record of the conversation. Records of enquiries that don't become projects are deleted within 12 months.

Who can see it

Only Harsh T, plus the services that deliver the message (such as the email provider, form service or booking tool).

This site

The site remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser. The footer's "now playing" status is fetched from a small server and sends no personal data.

Your rights

Email arnodelo4@gmail.com to see, correct or delete anything you've sent.
